02 / Context & task

One customer.
One connected view.

Customer details, subscription status and payment history often sit in separate tools. That makes even a routine support question harder to understand and resolve.

Subdesk explores a more direct workflow: search for a customer, see the subscription and payment context together, then follow a failed transaction to its details.

Product questionHow might a support agent move from a payment signal to a confident next step without losing the customer context?
03 / The approach

From signal
to resolution.

The interface is organised around a support workflow. Each step keeps the relevant customer and billing context close, while actions stay explicit and reviewable.

01 / FIND

Locate the customer

Search by name or email. Open a profile with account status, current plan, renewal details and payment history.

Customer details
02 / UNDERSTAND

Trace the payment

Review the transaction status and decline reason, then move between the payment and its related customer.

Transaction details
03 / ACT

Record a test refund

Admin confirms a full refund with a required reason. The transaction and activity log update together.

Refund → activity log

05 / Product decisions

Clarity in the details.

The demo focuses on legible context, a small role model and a reversible, local workflow rather than production payment infrastructure.

01 / CONTEXT

Keep customer and billing history connected

Transactions link back to the customer and subscription, making the next useful detail one step away.

02 / ACTION

Make the refund decision explicit

Only Admin can start a full test refund. The amount is fixed, a reason is required, and a second refund is rejected.

03 / RECORD

Update the payment and log together

The local data layer writes the refunded status and activity record in one IndexedDB transaction.

Implementation

A browser-based interactive demo.

How it works

There is no server, payment provider or real money movement. Fictional customers, subscriptions and transactions are stored locally in the browser. Demo role selection illustrates access differences; it is not server authentication.

Concept project · Fictional company and test data · No real customer payments
