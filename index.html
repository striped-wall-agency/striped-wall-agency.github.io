Why we focus on this

We've spent years building and running payment integrations and internal tools in live products.

We saw firsthand how much easier it became for support and operations teams to do their jobs when they could investigate a failed payment, issue a refund, or update an account on their own.

That experience is why we focus on SaaS payments and admin dashboards. We start by understanding how your team handles the work today, then build around what they need to get it done.

05 / About Striped Wall

Design and engineering, working as one.

In traditional workflows, design and development happen one after another. This can lead to rework when technical requirements meet design decisions.

At Striped Wall, both disciplines work in parallel from day one. Design decisions account for technical realities, while engineering evolves alongside the product vision.

Fewer compromises. Less rework. Better products.

One integrated product team. Two complementary areas of expertise.
